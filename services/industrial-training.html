Services / Industrial training

Practical training for the people who run the plant.

  • What it is

    Practical training in industrial automation and cybersecurity: how controllers and other OT hardware work, industrial software, protocols and networks, how OT differs from IT, and how to keep systems secure day to day.

  • Who it is for

    Operators, maintenance teams, automation engineers and IT staff who work with OT, from teams starting out to experienced engineers who want to add security to their work.

  • Why it is needed

    Many OT incidents start with people: a shared password, an unknown USB stick, a remote connection left open. Teams that understand their systems make fewer mistakes and spot problems sooner.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

Which topics can be covered?

OT hardware and controllers, industrial protocols and networks, PLC and HMI basics, how OT differs from IT, common attacks on industrial systems, secure working practices and incident response. Each programme is built from these topics to fit the team.

Is the training tailored?

Yes. Content, depth and examples are adapted to the team, the sector and the systems they work with.

Where does it take place?

On site or online. Format, language and duration are agreed with each customer.

Does it help with NIS2?

Yes. NIS2 requires cybersecurity training for staff and for management bodies, and the training can be part of the evidence for that requirement.

How long is a course?

From a short awareness session to several days of technical training, depending on the topics and the audience.
