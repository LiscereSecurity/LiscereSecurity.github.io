Services / OT visibility and monitoring

Know every device on your control network, and what it is doing.

  • What it is

    A passive study of the control network that delivers a complete OT asset inventory: every controller, workstation and device, the protocols in use and who writes to which points. It can be extended with a pilot of our framework, which flags actions that do not fit the state of the process.

  • Who it is for

    Plants and operators without an up to date inventory of their OT, companies preparing for NIS2 or an audit, and teams that want to see contextual anomaly detection working on their own network.

  • Why it is needed

    What is not known cannot be protected. Most sites have grown over years of projects and vendors, and the drawings rarely match reality. An accurate inventory is the base of every other security measure.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

What is an OT asset inventory?

A list of every device on the control network, such as controllers, HMIs, engineering workstations, switches and gateways, with vendor, model and firmware where the traffic reveals it, and a map of how they communicate.

Is anything installed on the network?

We connect to a mirror port on a switch and only observe a copy of the traffic. Nothing is scanned, injected or changed, and nothing sits in the control path.

What does the framework pilot add?

The inventory shows what is on the network. The pilot goes further: our framework learns the normal operation of the process and flags supervisory actions that do not fit its current state, with the evidence behind each verdict.

Which protocols are supported?

The inventory works from the traffic itself and covers the common industrial protocols, such as Modbus/TCP, S7comm, OPC UA, EtherNet/IP and PROFINET. The framework has been validated in our laboratory on Modbus/TCP and OPC UA, and each pilot starts by confirming the protocols in scope.

Does it help with NIS2?

Yes. An asset inventory is the starting point of the risk analysis NIS2 requires, and monitoring supports the detection and handling of incidents.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how long the process needs to be observed to see its normal operation. Scope, duration and price are agreed in a short first call and fixed up front.
