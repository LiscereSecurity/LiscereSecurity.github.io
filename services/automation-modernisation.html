Services / Review and modernisation

Make the automation you already have reliable and secure.

  • What it is

    An analysis of existing PLC and HMI software to find faults, undocumented logic, risks and security gaps, followed by the improvements needed or a migration from legacy platforms to current ones, planned around production.

  • Who it is for

    Plants running automation written years ago, often by people or companies no longer around, on platforms reaching end of life or with software nobody fully understands any more.

  • Why it is needed

    Old automation keeps running until it does not. Spare parts disappear, knowledge leaves with people, and legacy controllers lack basic protections. A review shows what is at risk before it fails.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

What does the software review cover?

The PLC and HMI programs and their documentation: logic structure, error handling, alarms, interlocks, unused or undocumented code, and the security settings of controllers and panels. Findings are ranked by their risk to the process.

Which legacy platforms can be migrated?

Common cases include Siemens S5 and S7-300/400 to S7-1500, Rockwell PLC-5 and SLC 500 to ControlLogix or CompactLogix, and old operator panels to current HMI and SCADA software. Other platforms are assessed case by case.

Can the migration be done without stopping production?

The work is planned around production: the new software is tested before the switch, and the changeover is scheduled for an agreed stop, such as a weekend or planned maintenance.

Is the original source code needed?

It helps but is not essential. Where the documentation or source is missing, the program can be read back from the controller and documented as part of the review.

How is this different from an OT security assessment?

The assessment looks at the whole OT environment from a security point of view. The review goes deep into the automation software itself: how it works, where it can fail and how to improve it.

How is the work priced?

After a first call and a look at the existing system, scope and price are agreed up front.
