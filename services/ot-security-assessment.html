Services / OT and ICS security assessment

Find the weak points in your OT before an attacker does.

  • What it is

    A structured review of controllers, operator stations, remote access and the networks between them. It uncovers vulnerabilities and security gaps, ranks them by their risk to the process and includes penetration testing on agreed systems.

  • Who it is for

    Manufacturing plants, utilities, energy, water and transport operators, and the integrators and machine builders that serve them. Any site where a control system runs a physical process.

  • Why it is needed

    Most OT networks were built to run, not to resist attacks. Controllers accept commands from anyone on the network, and remote access often reaches further than anyone thinks. An assessment finds those gaps first.

Questions we hear

Straight answers.

What does the assessment cover?

Controllers, operator stations and engineering workstations (vulnerabilities, configuration and firmware), the network architecture and segmentation between IT and OT, remote access for staff, vendors and integrators, and whether systems can be restored after an incident. Penetration testing is included on agreed systems.

Will the assessment disrupt production?

No. On live OT networks we observe rather than probe: nothing is scanned, injected or changed while the process is running. Penetration testing runs only on systems and in time windows agreed with your team.

Which systems and vendors are covered?

Industrial control systems from the major vendors, including Siemens, Rockwell Automation (Allen-Bradley), Schneider Electric, ABB, Beckhoff, Omron and Mitsubishi Electric, as well as systems built on CODESYS, and the SCADA, HMI and network equipment around them.

What is delivered at the end?

A report in plain language with every finding ranked by its risk to the process, the evidence attached and a practical roadmap of what to fix first and what can wait. The method is aligned with IEC 62443.

Does the assessment help with NIS2?

Yes. NIS2 requires risk analysis and security measures proportionate to the risk. The assessment provides the technical evidence for the OT side, and it can be combined with a full NIS2 readiness assessment.

How long does it take?

Typically a few weeks for a single site, depending on its size and the scope agreed in a short first call. Scope and price are fixed up front.
