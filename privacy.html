Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026

This site uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. This notice explains the little personal data that is processed, why, and your rights.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Liscere sp. z o.o. (LISCERE SPÓŁKA Z OGRANICZONĄ ODPOWIEDZIALNOŚCIĄ), ul. Prezydenta Gabriela Narutowicza 40/1, 90-135 Łódź, Poland, KRS 0001256054. For anything about your data, write to hello@liscere.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer, because the law does not require one for an organisation of our size and activity.

2. What personal data is processed

  • When you email us. If you write to hello@liscere.com, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, the content of your message and anything you attach, and the technical headers that come with any email.
  • When you visit the site. The site is a set of static pages. Like any web server, the hosting provider receives your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser's user agent and the referring page, and keeps these in server logs for security. We do not have access to these logs.
  • Fonts. The pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which receive your IP address, user agent and the address of the page.

We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, social media plugins, embedded third-party video or contact forms. The site's own scripts only animate the page and filter the research list; they do not collect or send anything. The demo video and the research PDFs are served from this site.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Replying to your email and any follow-up correspondence: our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Where you write to us about a possible contract, also steps taken at your request before entering into one (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Keeping the site secure and available, including the hosting provider's server logs: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Displaying the site in its intended typefaces through Google Fonts: our legitimate interest in a consistent presentation (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Keeping correspondence where the law requires it, for example for accounting or tax purposes once a business relationship exists: compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

You are not obliged to give us any personal data. If you do not email us, we hold none about you. We do not make decisions about you by automated means or profile you.

4. Who receives the data

  • GitHub, Inc. (United States), which hosts this site on GitHub Pages and processes visitor IP addresses and logs as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), which provides DNS for liscere.com. Cloudflare answers DNS lookups for the domain; it does not sit between you and the site and does not receive your page requests.
  • Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland, and its affiliate Apple Inc., United States), which hosts the hello@liscere.com mailbox on iCloud Mail.
  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC, United States), which serves the fonts through Google Fonts. We also use Google Search Console to see aggregated data on how the site appears in Google Search; Search Console adds no code to these pages and collects nothing from visitors.

Some of these providers process data in the United States, outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (GDPR Art. 46(2)(c)) and, where the provider is certified under it, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision (Art. 45). We do not sell personal data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

5. How long it is kept

  • Emails: for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up, and deleted no later than 24 months after the last message, unless they become part of a business relationship or are needed to establish or defend a legal claim, in which case they are kept for the period the law requires.
  • Server logs: kept by GitHub for the period set out in its privacy statement. We do not hold copies.
  • Font requests: logged by Google for the period set out in Google's own policies.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict its processing, to object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to data portability where the law provides it. To exercise any of these rights, write to hello@liscere.com. We will reply within one month, and we may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

7. Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl. You may also complain to the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live or work.

8. Cookies

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser (no local storage, session storage or similar). GitHub Pages sets no cookies on liscere.com, and Cloudflare, used for DNS only, is not in the path of your requests and sets none either. Google states that the Google Fonts service does not set cookies. Because nothing is stored on your device, there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

9. Changes

If what the site does with personal data changes, this notice will be updated first, and the date at the top will change.
