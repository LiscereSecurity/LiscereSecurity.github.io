3. Cookies and analytics

liscere.com does not set cookies and does not use tracking or advertising technologies.

To understand how the website is used, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, provided by Cloudflare, Inc. It does not use cookies or local storage and does not identify or follow individual visitors across websites. When a page loads, a small script from Cloudflare reports aggregate information such as the page viewed, the referring website, the country, the type of device and browser, and how fast the page loaded. Your IP address is used only to deliver this information and is not stored by Cloudflare for analytics. We use it on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the website useful and working well (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The typefaces used on this website are served from our own website. Apart from the analytics script described above, no fonts, scripts or other resources are loaded from third parties when a page is displayed. The only other connection to a third party happens when you send the contact form (see section 4).

4. Who receives your data

We do not sell or rent personal data. Your data may be handled on our behalf by service providers who are bound by contract to process it only on our instructions: our email and cloud hosting provider, the provider of our website hosting, the provider of the contact form service, Formspree, Inc. (United States), and the provider of our website analytics, Cloudflare, Inc. (United States). When you send the contact form, its content goes to Formspree, which forwards it to us by email and keeps a copy in our account; we delete those copies on the same schedule as our email. Where a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we rely on the safeguards provided for in the GDPR, such as an adequacy decision or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, for example to a court or a public authority with a lawful request.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed;
  • have your data erased, where there is no longer a lawful reason for us to keep it;
  • restrict processing in the situations set out in Art. 18 GDPR;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation;
  • receive the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used, machine readable format, where processing is based on a contract and carried out by automated means.

To exercise any of these rights, write to hello@liscere.com. We will answer within one month, or tell you if we need longer and why.

If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl. You may also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

6. Security

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including encrypted connections to this website, access limited to the people who need it, and the security practices we apply in our own work.

7. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our processing changes, for example if we add a contact form backend or website analytics. The version number and effective date at the top tell you which version applies. Material changes will be announced on this page.
