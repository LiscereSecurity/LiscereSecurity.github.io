What we do

Security that understands the process.

Liscere is an industrial cybersecurity company based in Łódź, Poland. We work on the question most security stacks never ask: is this action appropriate for the current state of the physical process? A command can pass every identity, access and protocol check and still be the wrong command. That research shapes everything we offer, from a first risk assessment to a passive monitoring pilot on your plant network.

Authorised is not always appropriate.

Industrial and OT security

For the network that moves physical things.

PLCs, HMIs, SCADA, drives, building and energy systems. We assess and observe them without ever sitting in the path of the process.

  • Assessment

    OT and ICS security assessment

    A structured review of your control environment: controllers, operator stations, engineering access, remote connections and the network between them. You get a prioritised list of exposures, explained against how your process actually runs.

    For plants, utilities, energy and building systems of any size.

  • Passive

    OT network visibility

    We connect a passive observer to a mirror port and reconstruct what talks to what, which protocols are in use, who writes to which points, and what normal looks like. No agents, no scans, nothing injected into the process.

    For teams that want an honest inventory before buying anything.

  • Pilot

    Contextual anomaly detection pilot

    A time-boxed pilot of Liscere's evaluation layer on your own network. It learns the process from traffic, then flags supervisory actions that do not fit the current state, with the evidence behind each verdict.

Cybersecurity foundations

The groundwork every industrial company needs.

Not every company starts with an OT monitoring project. Many start with a deadline, an audit request or a customer questionnaire. We cover that ground too.

  • Compliance

    NIS2 and KSC readiness

    A gap assessment against the obligations of the amended Polish KSC act, with an OT lens: information security management, incident handling, audit readiness and the technical evidence behind each. Obligations fall due by 3 April 2027, with the first mandatory audit for key entities by 3 April 2028.

    Deadline driven

    Read more
  • Assessment

    Cybersecurity risk assessment

    A structured gap assessment against a recognised framework such as ISO 27001 or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, covering IT and OT together so the two sides stop being assessed in isolation.

    For companies answering a board, an insurer or a customer.

    Ask about this
  • Design

    Security architecture review

    Segmentation between IT and OT, zones and conduits, remote access paths, backup and recovery of controllers and operator stations. We tell you what to fix first and what can wait.

    For new lines, retrofits and post-incident rebuilds.

    Ask about this
  • Readiness

    Incident response planning

    A response plan that fits a plant: who decides to stop a line, how to isolate without losing view, what evidence to keep, and how to report to the right CSIRT team on time.

    For companies that have a plan for IT and nothing for OT.

How we work

Three steps. No surprises.

  1. Scoping call

    Thirty minutes, free. You tell us what you run and what worries you. We tell you honestly whether we are the right fit and what a sensible first step looks like.

  2. Assessment or pilot

    Fixed scope and fixed price agreed up front. On OT networks we use passive methods only: nothing is scanned, injected or changed while the process is running.

  3. Report and roadmap

    Plain language, prioritised, with the evidence attached. We walk your team through it and stay available for the questions that come up afterwards.
